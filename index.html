Ragas, recordings and listening

Two things I've built around Hindustani classical music: an app that plays the right raga for the hour, and a catalog of the recordings I collect and stream.

Web app

Raga Vox

A live samay chakra, the traditional raga time wheel. It shows which ragas belong to this hour and plays a recording of one.

  • 25 ragas across the 8 prahars of the day, plus seasonal ragas like Basant and Megh in their months
  • Follows your local clock and says so when you're outside India, with a switch to India time
  • Plays Spotify recordings with no login; click any prahar to explore its ragas

Catalog

Mahesh's Music Archive

My collection of Hindustani classical recordings, mostly classic HMV/Saregama albums kept as lossless FLAC, with the story behind each one.

  • Search and filter by raga, artist, gharana, instrument, form and time of day
  • Liner notes, track-by-track raga details and a Spotify link for every album and track
  • Also shows albums I don't have yet, and curated streaming lists like 50 raga-based film songs
